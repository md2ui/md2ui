# Hero figure

**Use for:** one figure that leads the page. **Not for:** two to four equal figures (`kpis`), or a figure with its history (`chart-card`).

```markdown
## The Long Table

_Listeners this month_
___48,210___ - `▲ 6%`
_Episode 112 leads, with 9,400 plays_
```

**Why each marker**
- The title names what the figure belongs to: a show, an account, a city.
- A project may give the leading section its own style; its styles, if any, are in `guide("theme")`.
- The label, the figure and the line are glued: one group, read top down — `structure.md`.
- `___…___` is the Display tier, the largest thing on the page — `text.md`.
- The change is a chip after ` - `; a rise or a fall takes the theme's token.
- The last muted line says what the figure rests on, or its range: a high and a low, a period.
