# Guide

**Use for:** advice about several named things, each worth a sentence or two. **Not for:** items to compare side by side (`listing`, `comparison`).

```markdown
# A weekend in Harbor Point

Two days is enough for one neighbourhood on foot, one museum and a lot of food.

## Where to eat

**The Tin Kettle** has fried fish at the harbour wall since 1962. Order the half portion with mushy peas and eat it on the steps.

![Fish and chips](fish.jpg?md2ui.aspect_ratio=4:3,16:9@600)

**Saltbox** serves an oyster lunch that runs all afternoon. Book a day ahead.

## What to see

**The Lighthouse Museum** sells out days ahead, so buy the timed ticket before you travel.
```

**Why each marker**
- Each item is a sentence that opens with its **bold** name, so it stays prose: the reason to go is the content.
- `##` makes each part of the guide a section — `structure.md`.
- A picture sits under the item it shows, at the subject's ratio — `images.md`.
