## About

___#2,087___
_in the world_

__2,764,051 monthly listeners__

The Meadow Lamps are a four-piece indie-folk band who started out playing village halls in a river valley in the early 2000s. Ruth Calloway and Simon Achebe met at a weekend fiddle class when they were twelve, and spent their teens trading tapes and writing songs in Simon's parents' garage. Ruth's grandmother taught her the accordion, which is still on every record. Marta Velde joined on drums after the three of them shared a ferry home from a festival, and the line-up was settled when Owen Pryce answered a note on a café noticeboard asking for "a bass player who can also drive the van". Their first album was recorded in a disused chapel over a single wet fortnight, and the band still tracks its vocals in a church whenever it can find one that will have them.
