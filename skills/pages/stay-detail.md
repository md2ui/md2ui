## Neighborhood highlights

Kestrel Vale is a farming village in a broad green valley. It is known for its round church tower, built in 1712, and for a Sunday market of cider, honey and pies. A long ridge path leads to a ruined beacon above the village, with views across three counties. The old forge on the green now houses a small museum of farm tools. Beside it, a former granary shows work by local potters and weavers.
