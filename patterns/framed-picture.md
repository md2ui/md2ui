# Framed picture

**Use for:** one photo that matters as a keepsake: a picture the user pinned, shared or wants to keep. **Not for:** pictures that illustrate a list of items (`photo-card`, `picture-rows`), or a picture that is the background of a card.

```markdown
## A framed picture

### {#framed-1}

![Framed](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1551650975-87deedd944c3?q=80&w=1200)
```

**Why each marker**
- `{#framed-1}` on the `###` asks this theme for its framed card: the picture fills it to the edges, inside a dark outline with a hard offset shadow — `MEANINGS.md`.
- The `###` carries no title: the frame holds the picture alone, and the `##` above names it.
- One picture per frame; number each one: `{#framed-2}`.
