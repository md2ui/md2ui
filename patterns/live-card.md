# Live card

**Use for:** the single item in progress now: something playing, recording, running, trading or on its way. **Not for:** a status that is settled (done, closed, delivered), or a current value that is not changing in front of the user (`kpis`).

```markdown
## Your order {#dark-1}
`On its way`

___12 min___
_Driver two streets away_

*[📍 Track it](agent:track-order)*

## Earlier orders

- **Noodle bar** : _Friday, delivered_
- **Bakery** : _Tuesday, delivered_
```

**Why each marker**
- `{#dark-1}` asks this theme for its dark card; one per page, on the item in progress — `MEANINGS.md`.
- The time left leads in the Display tier, the detail under it as a caption — `text.md`.
- The action that follows it sits in the card. The finished items stay ordinary rows.
