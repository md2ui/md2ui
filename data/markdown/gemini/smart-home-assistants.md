To answer your first question: **yes, absolutely**. In the context of the data processing and Markdown validation we discussed, "datapoints" (individual units of information) are the foundation of change detection.

If you are using a **data quality framework** (like *pointblank* for R/Markdown or *Great Expectations* for Python), you can set "assertions" that flag whenever a value shifts by a specific amount, such as $+3$. In a standard **Markdown schema validator**, you can't usually detect *changes* (since schemas check structure, not history), but you can validate that a number stays within a specific range or follows a specific increment logic.
