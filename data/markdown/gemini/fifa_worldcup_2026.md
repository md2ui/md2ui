# ⚽ FIFA World Cup 2026: Tournament Overview


The **23rd FIFA World Cup** will be a historic event, marking the first time the tournament is co-hosted by three nations and the first to feature an expanded field of 48 teams.

## 🏆 Tournament Format
* **Teams:** 48 (Expanded from 32)
* **Total Matches:** 104
* **Group Stage:** 12 groups of 4 teams each.
* **Knockout Path:** * Top 2 teams from each group + 8 best 3rd-place teams advance to the **Round of 32**.
    * This is the first edition to include a Round of 32.
* **Match Load:** Finalists will now play **8 matches** (up from 7) to win the trophy.

## 🏟️ Signature Venues
* **Opening Match Venue:** **Estadio Azteca (Mexico City Stadium)**. It makes history as the first stadium to host the opening match of three different World Cups (1970, 1986, 2026).
* **Final Match Venue:** **MetLife Stadium (New York New Jersey Stadium)**. Located in East Rutherford, NJ, it will host the grand finale on July 19.
* **Bronze Final (3rd Place):** Hard Rock Stadium (Miami Stadium).
* **Most Matches:** **Dallas Stadium** (AT&T Stadium) will host 9 matches, more than any other venue.

## 📍 Key Match Highlights (Group Stage)
* **June 11:** Mexico vs. South Africa (Mexico City)
* **June 12:** Canada starts their campaign in Toronto.
* **June 12:** USA starts their journey in Los Angeles (SoFi Stadium).
* **Simultaneous Play:** To ensure fairness, the final matches in each group will be played at the same time.
