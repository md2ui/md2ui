## Neighborhood highlights

Alder Falls is a small river village at the edge of a long valley. It is known for its stone bridge, built in 1790, and for a Saturday market of local cheese and bread. A steep trail climbs to an old watchtower above the falls, with views over the whole valley. The former mill by the bridge now holds a small museum of village history. Next to it, a converted barn shows work by painters from the region.
