# Climate Change Statistics (2014-2024)

## Temperature
- **Global average temperature** has risen approximately **1.1-1.2°C** above pre-industrial levels
- The last **10 years include the hottest years** ever recorded
- **2023** was the hottest year on record, about **1.48°C** above pre-industrial baseline

## Atmospheric CO₂
- CO₂ levels increased from **~398 ppm (2014)** to **~420 ppm (2024)**
- This represents the **highest concentration in at least 3 million years**

## Extreme Weather Events
- Significant increases in:
  - **Heatwaves**: longer, more frequent, and more intense
  - **Wildfires**: major events in Australia, California, Canada, Mediterranean
  - **Flooding**: increased by ~134% globally in frequency
  - **Hurricanes**: more Category 4-5 storms in Atlantic

## Ice & Sea Levels
- **Arctic sea ice**: declining ~13% per decade
- **Antarctic ice sheet**: losing ~150 billion tons/year
- **Glaciers**: lost trillions of tons of ice
- **Sea level rise**: increased ~3.3-3.7 mm/year (accelerating)

## Impacts
- **1 billion+ people** affected by climate-related disasters
- Economic losses from climate events: **hundreds of billions annually**
