# Popular Mexican Dishes

Here are some of the most popular and iconic Mexican dishes:

## 1. **Tacos**
Soft or crispy tortillas filled with various meats, vegetables, and toppings.
![Tacos](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1565299585323-38d6b0865b47?w=800)

## 2. **Enchiladas**
Rolled tortillas filled with meat or cheese, covered in chili sauce.
![Enchiladas](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1619681428730-e2d8a5ab3e6f?w=800)

## 3. **Guacamole**
Creamy avocado dip with lime, onions, tomatoes, and cilantro.
![Guacamole](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1603072617808-3e1d5e4f9e3e?w=800)

## 4. **Quesadillas**
Grilled tortillas filled with cheese and other ingredients.
![Quesadillas](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1618040996337-56904b7850b9?w=800)

## 5. **Pozole**
Traditional hominy soup with meat, typically served with toppings.
![Pozole](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1623601522394-9043a4e0e5e1?w=800)

## 6. **Chiles Rellenos**
Poblano peppers stuffed with cheese or meat, then fried.
![Chiles Rellenos](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1599038349548-e3bb4a21cc37?w=800)

## 7. **Tamales**
Corn dough filled with meats or sweets, steamed in corn husks.
![Tamales](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1618133187628-0c8c85fcf716?w=800)

## 8. **Mole**
Rich, complex sauce made with chocolate and chilies, served over meat.
![Mole](https://images.unsplash.com/photo-1617343267937-53de2c14d945?w=800)
