# Samsung The Frame TV 2026 (LS03H) - Comprehensive Review

**Model:** Samsung The Frame (LS03H) & The Frame Pro (LS03HW)
**Release Date:** January 2026 (Announced at CES 2026)  
**Review Date:** February 2026

---

## Executive Summary

Samsung's 2026 iteration of The Frame TV represents a strategic expansion rather than a revolutionary redesign. While some enthusiasts hoping for OLED or QD-OLED panels may feel disappointed, Samsung has doubled down on what made The Frame successful in the first place: being a lifestyle TV that prioritizes design, versatility, and smart integration over bleeding-edge picture technology.

**The Bottom Line:** The Frame 2026 gets smarter, bigger, and more flexible—but not sharper. For buyers who value aesthetics and ambient display as much as viewing quality, this evolution makes sense.

## What's New in 2026?

### Expanded Size Options

The most significant change is the dramatic expansion of available sizes:

**The Frame (LS03H):**
- 43" (new)
- 50" (new)  
- 55"
- 65"
- 75"
- 85"
- **98"** (new flagship size)

**The Frame Pro (LS03HW):**
- **55"** (new entry option)
- 65"
- 75"
- 85"

The addition of a massive 98-inch option brings The Frame into ultra-premium territory, competing with Samsung's high-end TV offerings while maintaining its art-focused identity. On the other end, the 43-inch model makes The Frame accessible for smaller spaces like bedrooms and offices.

### Connectivity Changes

Samsung has made some controversial connectivity decisions for 2026:

- **43" and 50" models:** Include the traditional wired One Connect Box
- **55" through 85" models:** Built-in ports only—no One Connect Box compatibility
- **98" model:** Ships with bezel included in the box (a premium touch)
- **Frame Pro (all sizes):** Features the new **Wireless One Connect Box** with Wi-Fi 7

The removal of One Connect Box support for mid-size models is a notable step backward for those who appreciated the cable management benefits. However, The Frame Pro's wireless solution represents the future Samsung envisions.

### Software & AI Integration

Both The Frame and Frame Pro now run **Tizen OS 10.0** with Samsung's One UI design, representing the company's most advanced smart TV platform to date.

**Key Software Features:**
- **Samsung Vision AI Companion:** An intelligent assistant that can answer questions, understand on-screen content, learn your preferences, and offer personalized recommendations for music, video, and even meals
- **7 Years of Software Updates:** Industry-leading support ensuring longevity
- **Multiple AI Assistants:** Bixby, Microsoft Copilot, and Perplexity integration
- **Enhanced Art Store:** Access to over 5,000 curated artworks from museums and galleries worldwide

## Display Technology

### The Frame (LS03H) - QLED

The standard Frame continues to use Samsung's QLED panel with traditional LED backlighting. While this isn't cutting-edge in 2026, it remains perfectly adequate for its intended use case.

**Panel Specs:**
- **Resolution:** 4K UHD (3840 x 2160)
- **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz
- **HDR Support:** HDR10, HDR10+, HDR10+ Advanced, HLG
- **Audio:** Dolby Atmos, Eclipsa Audio (new spatial sound system)
- **Anti-Glare:** Glare Free coating

### The Frame Pro (LS03HW) - Neo QLED

The Frame Pro offers a significant step up with Mini LED technology for improved contrast and brightness.

**Panel Specs:**
- **Technology:** Neo QLED with edge-mounted Mini LEDs
- **Resolution:** 4K UHD (3840 x 2160)
- **Refresh Rate:** 144Hz
- **Processor:** NQ4 Gen 3 AI Processor (same as flagship models)
- **HDR Support:** HDR10, HDR10+, HDR10+ Advanced, HLG
- **Audio:** Dolby Atmos, Eclipsa Audio
- **Anti-Glare:** Glare Free coating

The Neo QLED panel provides noticeably better brightness and contrast, making it the superior choice for rooms with lots of natural light or for viewers who prioritize picture quality alongside design.

For casual and competitive gamers alike, The Frame 2026 delivers a solid gaming experience that doesn't compromise when you switch from art display to gaming station.

## The Wireless One Connect Box (Frame Pro Only)

One of the most exciting innovations in The Frame Pro is the completely wireless One Connect Box—a first for Samsung.

**Features:**
- **Wi-Fi 7 Connectivity:** Latest wireless standard for reliable, high-bandwidth streaming
- **High-Compression Codec:** Enables wireless transmission of 4K content with minimal latency
- **Wall Penetration:** Works even with walls between the box and TV (up to 10 meters)
- **Cable-Free Installation:** Only power cable runs to the TV
- **Compact Design:** Smaller than previous wired models

This is a game-changer for installation, eliminating the single cable that connected previous One Connect boxes to the TV. For wall-mounted setups, this means a truly clean, gallery-like appearance.

## Audio Performance

### Built-in Speakers

Both Frame models feature down-firing speakers with the following technologies:

- **Dolby Atmos:** Immersive surround sound virtualization
- **Eclipsa Audio:** Samsung's new 3D spatial audio system (2026)
- **Object Tracking Sound:** Audio follows on-screen action
- **Adaptive Sound Pro:** Optimizes audio based on room acoustics and content
- **Q-Symphony:** Syncs with compatible Samsung soundbars for enhanced audio

**AI Sound Controller (New for 2026):** Allows fine-tuning of individual audio elements—voice, music, and effects can be adjusted separately for a customized listening experience.

### Audio Reality Check

While the built-in audio is adequate for casual viewing and certainly better than many TVs, serious movie watchers and audiophiles will want to pair The Frame with a quality soundbar. The slim design doesn't leave much room for powerful speakers, so external audio is highly recommended.

## Picture Quality Assessment

### Standard Viewing

For everyday TV watching, streaming content, and casual viewing, The Frame (QLED) performs admirably:

**Strengths:**
- Excellent color reproduction thanks to Quantum Dot technology
- Good brightness for well-lit rooms
- Glare Free coating genuinely helps in bright environments
- Solid upscaling of HD content to 4K

**Limitations:**
- Black levels don't match OLED or even high-end Mini LED TVs
- Viewing angles are acceptable but not exceptional
- Contrast ratio is good but not outstanding

### The Frame Pro Advantage

The Mini LED backlighting in the Frame Pro provides noticeable improvements:

- **Better Local Dimming:** Deeper blacks and improved contrast
- **Higher Peak Brightness:** More impactful HDR performance
- **Improved Uniformity:** More consistent brightness across the screen

For those prioritizing picture quality, the Frame Pro is worth the premium.

### HDR Performance

Both models support the latest HDR formats, including Samsung's proprietary HDR10+ Advanced, which promises enhanced brightness and color accuracy. However, like all Samsung TVs, The Frame lineup notably excludes Dolby Vision support—a decision that remains controversial among enthusiasts.

## Common Questions

### Do I need the Art Store subscription?

**No.** While the Art Store offers a vast library of premium artwork, Samsung provides a rotating selection of 30 free pieces per month. Additionally, you can upload your own photos or download artwork from free sources online.

### Can I use it with my existing soundbar?

**Yes.** The Frame supports Q-Symphony with Samsung soundbars and works with any soundbar via HDMI ARC/eARC or optical connection.

### Does Art Mode use a lot of electricity?

Art Mode uses minimal power compared to active viewing. The brightness sensor ensures the display only uses as much power as needed based on ambient light. You can also set a schedule for Art Mode to turn off during certain hours.

### Can I change the bezels easily?

**Yes.** Bezels attach magnetically and can be swapped in seconds. Samsung offers multiple styles and colors, and third-party options are also available.

### Is the wireless One Connect Box really wireless?

**Almost.** The TV itself still requires a power cable, but all signal transmission (HDMI, etc.) happens wirelessly between the One Connect Box and the TV. This is Frame Pro only.

This TV is not for everyone—and that's by design. The Frame occupies a unique niche in the market, prioritizing form and function in equal measure. If you've been waiting for The Frame to go OLED, you'll be waiting another year. But if you appreciate what The Frame represents—a TV that respects your living space—the 2026 model is the most refined version yet.

**Who wins with The Frame 2026?** People who understand that the best TV isn't always the one with the most advanced panel technology, but the one that best fits their life and living space.
