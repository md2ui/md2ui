# Top 10 Restaurants in New York City (2026)

Based on current dining guides and reviews from February 2026.

## 1. Adda (East Village)

Indian spot featuring lamb butter-heavy dishes and a plume of spices, currently ranked as one of the city's best. Highlights include fiery lamb parcha with lamb butter and zingy chutney-filled chaats.

## 2. Ci Siamo (Manhattan)

Italian restaurant topping Yelp's 2026 list, known for its rigatoni alla gricia and wood-fired Florentine steaks. The gricia is described as salty, cheesy, and velvety.

## 3. Bong

Vibrant Khmer restaurant with bold flavors from lemongrass, galangal, and chiles grown by the owners' family. Standout dishes include whole lobster with shallots and ginger, heritage pork chop, and clams machew in tamarind-lemongrass broth.

## 4. Lei (Chinatown)

Wine bar with Chinese American food located next to Nom Wah Tea Parlor. Features unique dishes like Jinhua ham with seasonal fruit, Chinese omelet with aged white jade radish, and sesame shao bing with butter. The wine selection spans the globe.

## 5. Ramen by Ra (East Village)

Breakfast-style ramen with unique twists like bacon, soy egg, and cheese (BEC) or lox and cream cheese over shoyu broth. Reservations required and released in two-week blocks on the 1st and 15th of each month at 9am.

## 6. The Modern (Midtown/MoMA)

Two-Michelin-star restaurant at 9 West 53rd Street delivering creative cooking in a sleek setting with sculpture garden views. Known for dishes like kanpachi crudo and attentive service.

## 7. Gabriel Kreuther (Midtown)

Contemporary French-American with Alsatian roots at 41 West 42nd Street. Offers refined but comfortable dining with warm service. Dishes include citrus-marinated hamachi and expertly crafted French-American cuisine.

## 8. Craft

Tom Colicchio's ingredient-driven restaurant participating in Restaurant Week for the first time. Known for consistent quality over decades, featuring dishes like wild arugula salad and ricotta agnolotti with short rib ragout.

## 9. Bourbon Steak (JW Marriott Essex House)

Michael Mina's modern steakhouse offering accessible fine dining. Restaurant Week menu includes unique items like Chips and Caviar Dip and big eye tuna 'foie-poivre'.

## 10. LenLen (Flatiron District)

Playful modern Thai at 40 East 20th Street. The name comes from a Thai phrase meaning "to play, lightly." Features party-like ambiance and dishes like Salmon Yum with seasonal fruit, cherry tomatoes, onion, and chili.

- **Khmer and Southeast Asian cuisines** are having a major moment
- **Creative ramen concepts** with breakfast-inspired twists
- **Wine bars with unique perspectives** on global selections
- **All-day brunch spots** and **Japanese-inspired cuisine** are trending
- Mix of established fine dining and exciting new casual concepts
