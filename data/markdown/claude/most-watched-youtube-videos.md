## 1. Baby Shark Dance 🦈

![Baby Shark Dance Thumbnail](https://img.youtube.com/vi/XqZsoesa55w/maxresdefault.jpg)

**Channel:** Pinkfong Baby Shark - Kids' Songs & Stories  
**Views:** 16.5 billion  
**Published:** June 17, 2016  
**Duration:** 2:17

**Description:** The undisputed king of YouTube. This children's song became a global phenomenon, becoming the first video to reach 10 billion views in January 2022. The catchy tune and simple dance moves have made it endlessly rewatchable for young children worldwide.

**Milestones:**
- First video to reach 8 billion views (February 2021)

- First video to reach 10 billion views (January 2022)

## Why These Videos Succeed

### 1. **Repeatability**
Children's songs and catchy music encourage multiple views from the same users.

### 2. **Short Duration**
Most top videos are under 5 minutes, making them easy to watch repeatedly.

### 3. **Universal Appeal**
Music and children's content transcend language barriers.

### 4. **Algorithm Optimization**
High completion rates and engagement signal quality to YouTube's recommendation system.

### 5. **Cultural Moments**
Songs tied to major events (like See You Again with Fast & Furious) gain emotional significance.

---

## How YouTube Counts Views

To count as a legitimate view:
- User must intentionally initiate playback
- Must watch at least 30 seconds (or entire video if shorter)
- Replays count, but limited to 4-5 views per IP per 24 hours
- YouTube's algorithm filters out bot views and suspicious activity
